Delete your account

You can request deletion of your Redemption account and associated personal data by emailing us from the address connected to your account.

  1. Email domagoj.jugovic74@gmail.com with the subject “Redemption account deletion request”.
  2. Send the request from your account email address and include your Redemption username.
  3. We may ask for information needed to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

We will delete or de-identify account information and content where technically feasible, subject to legal obligations and legitimate security needs. Some information can remain in protected backups for a limited period. This process is described in the Privacy Policy.

Important: account deletion must also be available from within the app before the app is submitted to the App Store. This webpage provides a public alternative and contact channel; it does not replace the in-app deletion control.
