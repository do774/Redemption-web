Community Guidelines

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Redemption is for honest reflection, repair and encouragement. We expect people to treat one another with care, even when conversations are difficult.

Be respectful

Do not harass, shame, bully, threaten, demean or target anyone. Disagreement is welcome when it stays humane; attacks on people are not.

Keep people safe

Do not post content that encourages self-harm, violence, dangerous conduct, illegal activity or exploitation. Do not share another person’s personal information, private messages, images or identifying details without clear permission.

Do not post hateful or sexual content

Content attacking people on the basis of protected characteristics, sexually explicit material, exploitation and content involving minors are not allowed.

Be real and accountable

Do not impersonate people, manipulate engagement, spread deliberate misinformation, scam others or use the service for spam. Share only content you have a right to share.

Reporting and enforcement

Use the in-app reporting controls when available to flag a post, comment or account that may violate these guidelines. Include enough context for us to assess the issue. For urgent concerns or support, email domagoj.jugovic74@gmail.com. We may remove content, limit reach, suspend accounts or take other action under our Terms of Use.

A note on support

Redemption is not a crisis service. If someone faces an immediate risk of harm, contact local emergency services or a qualified crisis resource rather than relying on a report in the app.
